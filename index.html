02 — Approach

I started drawing before I designed, and shipping before I strategized. The next shift is agentic AI — not a shortcut around craft, but a new material to design with: systems that act on people's behalf, and have to earn the right to do it.

Pixel-perfect screens→Decisions people can trust
Manual prototyping→AI-augmented prototyping
Guess and refine→Prototype and learn, faster with AI
Interface design→Agentic experience (AX) design
Reactive tickets→Proactive, explainable systems
Doing the work→Owning the business outcome

05 — How I work

Curious. Considered. AI-accelerated.

01

Sits with the problem.

Discovery and workflow analysis before drawing a single screen — the approach behind Intelligent DSRR, an AI-driven release-readiness system built to replace guesswork with evidence.

02

Designs for trust.

Treats explainability as a design requirement, not an afterthought — the real problem behind Intelligent DSRR was never the layout, it was earning trust in an AI-generated readiness score.

03

Sketches to think.

Wireframes and user flows before pixels — the process behind reworking a business-service insights experience that was previously unreadable to the people who needed it most.

04

Moves faster with AI.

Uses AI-augmented workflows to speed up prototyping and iteration — idea to testable prototype, faster — paired with an active practice in agentic experience (AX) design, framing decisions around measurable business outcomes.

05

Ships to learn.

Prototypes and shipped experiences on a cloud collaboration platform used by 80,000+ enterprise users — built, tested, and iterated in the open.
