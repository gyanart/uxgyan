05 — How I work

Research-led. Strategic. Still ships.

01

Starts with research.

Treats research as the foundation, not a formality — stakeholder interviews and workflow analysis before drawing a single screen, the approach behind Intelligent DSRR, an AI-driven release-readiness system built to replace guesswork with evidence.

02

Connects design to strategy.

Building formal fluency in framing UX decisions around measurable business outcomes — the step from executing design to shaping product direction, not just the screens that follow from it.

03

Designs for trust.

Treats explainability as a design requirement, not an afterthought — the real problem behind Intelligent DSRR was never the layout, it was earning trust in an AI-generated readiness score.

04

Sketches to think.

Wireframes and user flows before pixels — the process behind reworking a business-service insights experience that was previously unreadable to the people who needed it most.

05

Ships to learn.

Prototypes and shipped experiences on a cloud collaboration platform used by 80,000+ enterprise users, increasingly built with AI-augmented workflows to speed up iteration.

06

Makes the team better.

Mentors junior designers and contributes to UX quality, consistency, and design direction across cross-functional teams.
